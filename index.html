Viam: Robotics Hackathon - AutoWelder

A pick and place station for welders

Before steel can be welded, the pieces have to be lined up closely enough for a welder to tack them. The robot maps out the environment using the point cloud from the camera, so the cubes and steel can go anywhere in the workspace. A new table layout only needs a fresh scan.

On the dashboard, you select the cubes where you want the steel, and the robot places it. It grips the steel at its centre and sets it across the cubes from camera readings, not hand-taught paths. For the second steel, you select the cubes as well as the steel you want to weld it to, and the robot places it automatically.

As it places the second steel, the robot reads the gap between the two steels from the camera image, as a dip in brightness across the joint, and nudges the steel closer, 1 mm or less at a time, until there is no space left.

Results

  • Full cycle passed 10/10 on each of 4 table layouts on the real arm
  • Picks passed 10/10 from random starts in Isaac Sim
  • Cut drift per cycle from 19 mm to 7.5 mm by tracing it to an off-centre grip
  • Two camera looks at a steel before the grab agreed to 0.36 mm on average (13 runs)

Hardware

  • UFACTORY xArm 850 robot arm
  • RealSense D435 depth camera, mounted on the wrist

Software

  • Viam
  • Python
  • React and Three.js dashboard
