About CV

I build machine learning systems and the teams that ship them.

I'm an ML engineer and engineering manager. I lead engineering at FINESSE, a Series A startup in Los Angeles, where I work on machine learning and AI agents.

Before that I led the motion planning team at Imperial Driverless, which finished in the top five of the team's first competition with a real-time perception and SLAM stack on a Jetson-powered race car. I studied Mathematics with Economics at the London School of Economics, then an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Imperial College London, where my thesis used reinforcement learning to generate safety-critical scenarios for autonomous vehicles.

Most recently, at the VibeCon 2026 hackathon, I built Auto Parts Finder, an AI voice agent that phones car dealerships to get part prices and stock for mechanics.
